How they were built

Motion

  • Brand curves as GSAP CustomEases: cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), (.16,1,.3,1) and (.77,0,.175,1).
  • Three brand transitions: a diagonal band wipe in sky, teal and navy on the 45° line of the mark, a chamfered block wipe, and a zoom-through.
  • Nothing bounces, shakes, glitches or whip-pans.

The coin and imagery

  • The real site coin as a transparent 6s turn, driven by HyperFrames speed ramps for the spin-ins.
  • Glass sculptures, macro textures and aurora grounds from the brand kit.
  • Reel 5 flies a 3D camera past five cards, computed as a pure function of time so every frame is reproducible.

Sound

  • A 120 BPM bed per reel with kicks, risers, impacts, count ticks, notification dings and an end chord placed on the cut grid.
  • Synthesised in Python, so it is royalty-free and can be rebuilt with _tools/synth.py.
  • Normalised to -14 LUFS for Reels and TikTok.
