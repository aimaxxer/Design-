Essentials

Say it like a desk, not a promoter

Institutional and calm, sharp on social as long as every claim holds up. Speak to the partner as “you”, about “your clients” and “your flow”. We are “we”.

Always
IB’s

Capital IB, curly apostrophe, lowercase s. It stays lowercase inside uppercase headlines too: type the headline in capitals and leave All Caps off.

Never
IBS

An All Caps style or text transform turns IB’s into IBS. Check every headline, pill and caption before export.

Writing rules

  • Headlines UPPERCASE; body in sentence case.
  • Short declarative sentences. Periods do the work.
  • No em or en dashes. Ranges take a hyphen: 2-3x.
  • Lead with the money, then the mechanism.
  • One number per frame. Examples are labelled “Example”.

Claims we can make

  • +40% average rebate increase
  • 2-3x more rebates per 10X Trader client
  • Offer within 1 hour of the strategy call
  • No fees. The broker pays us, not you.
  • 10X Trader: 1:9 credit, so 10x buying power

Never write

  • bonus, free, guaranteed, risk-free
  • a profit promise or a credit cap
  • elevate, unlock your potential, game-changer, passive income
  • emoji or hashtags inside the design

Any frame that explains 10X Trader mechanics carries the risk line: “Leveraged trading carries a high level of risk. 10X Trader is not available in the US.”

Logo

The mark and the lockup

The 10X mark beside “affiliation” over “partner program”, exactly as in the post header. Text is outlined in every file, so nothing depends on fonts.

Clear space and size

Keep the height of the 10X mark clear on every side (the hatched band). In a 1080-wide post the mark is 38px tall, top-left at 80px from the edge, 70px from the top. Smallest on screen: mark 16px tall.

In motion

  • The lockup arrives with the header: fade and rise 10px, 1s, starting 0.3s in.
  • It never spins, bounces, scales up or animates letter by letter.
  • The 10X coin is the only 3D or moving form of the mark.
  • White lockup on navy, deep and dark imagery; navy lockup on light grounds. Never teal, never sky.
  • For After Effects, import the PDFs in logo/pdf and turn on Continuously Rasterize. PNGs at 2600px are in logo/png.

The 10X coin

The hero object, from the site’s own code

Rendered from the 10X Trader site’s three.js coin: engraved glass, brand sky tint, studio light. Use it as the one hero per frame, at 55-75% of the short side alone or 30-40% beside a headline.

2048px transparent PNGs in coin/stills. The same poses are in the posts.

Files for motion

  • coin/turn/: the 6-second seamless turn as 180 transparent PNGs at 1080px, 30 fps. Import as a PNG sequence.
  • coin/video/coin-turn-alpha-1080.webm: the same turn with alpha (VP9), for web and Remotion.
  • coin/video/coin-turn-navy-1080.mp4, -frost-1080.mp4 and coin-turn-story-1080x1920.mp4: ready on a ground.
  • coin-studio/index.html: open it in Chrome to render any pose, size (up to 2048), length, frame rate, tint or finish as a PNG sequence. It runs offline.

Coin rules

  • Turn: linear, 6s per turn (14s when idling). Intro: one turn in 1.6s, smoothstep, ending still.
  • Float: 5.2% of its radius, one gentle sine per loop. Lean -12°, tipped 6° toward you.
  • It may turn, float and catch light. It never bounces, squashes, rains, stacks like casino chips or explodes.
  • On navy, deep, frost, mist and white. Not on photos; one coin per frame as the hero.

Using imagery in motion

  • Inside a mask, imagery settles from 114% to 100% over 1.8s while the mask holds.
  • Full-bleed grounds drift slowly: 100% to 104% over the clip, linear.

Rules for every edit

  • 30 fps for social, 60 fps for showreels.
  • Linear only for loops: the coin turn and ticker bands.
  • Hold each message at least 1.5s after it lands.
  • One hero move per frame: the coin, a number or the headline.
  • Camera moves use ease-in-out-strong. No whip pans or glitches.

After Effects

  • Install the five Inter fonts from fonts/.
  • File › Scripts › Run Script File › motion/10x-brand-comps.jsx. It builds post, square, story and landscape comps with the navy ground, sample type, the coin and safe-zone guides.
  • Import coin/turn as a PNG sequence at 30 fps; add loopOut("cycle") on Time Remap.
  • Paste curves and count-ups from motion/after-effects-expressions.md.

Premiere Pro, Resolve, CapCut

  • Premiere and Resolve import numbered PNG sequences with alpha; use the MP4s on a ground anywhere else.
  • Set the sequence to 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps and lay the safe-zone PNG on the top track, disabled for export.

Figma, Jitter, Rive, Lottie

  • Paste the bezier numbers into the custom easing field.
  • Variables and styles come from tokens/tokens.json; CSS projects can link tokens/tokens.css.
